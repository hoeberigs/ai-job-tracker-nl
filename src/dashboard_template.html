Method

How the numbers are made

A scheduled job collects listings from __SOURCES__ once a day. Each ad is matched against keyword lists for role, seniority, work arrangement and skills. No language model is involved, so an ad that phrases a skill in an unusual way is missed.

An ad that stays open is seen again every day. The __TOTAL_OBSERVATIONS__ daily sightings collapse to __TOTAL_POSTINGS__ distinct ads by URL, and employer counts use distinct ads.

Skill shares use only ads whose full text could be retrieved, because a title alone rarely names a skill. Days with fewer than 30 such ads carry no share, which is why the skills figure starts later than the first run. The headline and the change column compare four-week averages, so they differ slightly from the 7-day line ends. The 3-point cut-off that colours a line is a reading aid, not a significance test.
